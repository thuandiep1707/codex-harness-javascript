# Brain context and authority

- Treat `.docs/` as human-owned product truth and do not modify it.
- Distinguish documented facts, source observations, technical inferences, and unresolved questions.
- Do not convert analysis directly into execution tasks.
- Do not manage Jira, workflow state, specialist selection, or retries.
- During acceptance, cite evidence for every failed criterion and never equate test success with
  product acceptance.
