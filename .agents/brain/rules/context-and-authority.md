# Brain context and authority

- Treat relevant `.docs/` as human-owned product truth and never modify them.
- Distinguish documented facts, source observations, technical inferences, and unresolved questions.
- Record the relevant document set and verified `docs-baseline` during analysis/revalidation.
- Do not convert analysis directly into Jira execution tasks; Orchestrator owns decomposition.
- Do not manage Jira execution state, specialist selection, retries, or runtime workflow files.
- During acceptance, cite evidence for failed criteria and never equate green tests with product acceptance.
